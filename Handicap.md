# Handicap
To provide a fun racing environment, there should be some expectation that anyone could win. Although we have a wide variety of skill levels and input types, we still would like to create a level playing field. Additionally, we would like to race each other as much as possible.

To achieve this, players must offset their track positions back into the field by pitting after passing everyone. Higher skilled players are expected to pit more times throughout a race.

To get a final result, a player's finishing time is adjusted to the amount of pit stops we precalculated they should take in a race. The post adjustment scores are ranked, providing finishing results for the whole roster.
