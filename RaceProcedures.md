# Race Procedures

1. Default setups must be used.
1. Do not abuse the lack of physical damage for lap time or position.
1. When nearing the pit entrance, if you are near the front of the bot pack, you must pit.
    * DO NOT TAKE A LAP OF CLEAN AIR OUT IN FRONT!
    * This does not change your race outcome, only your track position.
    * There is a chance of getting stuck or even DNF a race by entering the pits wrong, due to the automated setting. While we do not enforce penalties, speeding and unsafe entries into the pit lane can be dangerous to your race.

1. When racing other players...
    * Race cleanly and fairly. Leave space if you think a driver could be there.
    * Assume nobody is at fault for accidents until after the race review.
    * Fault accidents are learning opportunities, not blame opportunities.
    * It is up to the attacking driver to find a safe opportunity to overtake.
    * Appreciate the challenge your opponent provides.
