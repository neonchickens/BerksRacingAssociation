# Host Settings

1. Map and Track selection
    1. The host always selects the car and track combination.

1. Realism Settings
    1. Default assumption is whatever reset to default says.
    1. Disable object collision should be yes.
    1. Damage type should be visual only.
    1. Pit stop errors should be no.
    1. Allow vehicle reset should be yes.

1. Host Settings
    1. Privacy should be "Friends Only"
        * Private will disallow mid round joins. This game can be difficult occaisionally so we really recommend friends only.

1. Practice
    1. Not required but the game can be difficult occaisionally. Allow some time and forgiveness for failures.

1. Qualifying
    1. Default assumption is whatever reset to default says.
    1. The qualifying session will be 10 minutes in length.
    1. Livetrack preset should be heavy rubber.
    1. The qualifying should be private.

1. Race
    1. Default assumption is whatever reset to default says.
    1. The race length in laps will be an estimate to 40 minutes.
    1. The starting order will be decided by a qualifying session.
    1. Start type should be rolling.
    1. Livetrack preset should be heavy rubber.

1. Opponents
    1. Default assumption is whatever reset to default says.
    1. Grid size will be maxed out (track specific, double check).
    1. Make sure there is room for everyone in max human opponents.
    1. Fill with AI opponents should be on.
    1. Opponent skill level should be based on the slowest racer's best practice times.
    1. AI aggression should be maximum.
       * Prevents AI from jumping out of the way, into somebody else. More realistic to hold your ground anyway.
    1. Opponent field type may be identical or same class depending on the car selection.

1. Rules & Penalties
    1. Default assumption is whatever reset to default says.
    1. Rules & Penalties should be off.
